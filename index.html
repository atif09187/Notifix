About this app

Notifox helps you keep track of your notifications in one convenient place. View saved notification history, organize notifications by application and revisit supported notifications whenever you need them.

Designed as a dedicated Android notification tracker and notification saver, Notifox actively monitors the device status bar via Android’s native listener service. Whether you inadvertently swipe away a critical message or want a searchable notification log for communication apps, Notifox ensures that captured notifications remain accessible locally on your handset.

Implemented Notification Management Features

Straightforward, privacy-conscious notification tracking designed for your everyday Android workflow.

Comprehensive Notification History

Records incoming system notifications systematically so you can easily review dismissed alerts and read missed messages at any time using a dedicated Android notification log.

WhatsApp Notification Saver

Preserves notification alerts delivered by WhatsApp while notification access is enabled. If a sender deletes a message after notification delivery, the notification preview originally captured by Notifox remains preserved in your local history.

Social Media Notification History

Maintains clear incoming alerts from Telegram, Instagram, Messenger, and other supported platforms whenever notifications are received by your Android status bar.

App-Wise Notification Manager

Filter alerts directly by their source application. Sort your notification log by specific app names to locate the exact information you require with zero confusion.

Date-Wise Notification Tracker

Browse through chronological records indexed by calendar date, allowing you to trace what was received today, yesterday, or days earlier on your Android device.

Background Notification Listener

Operates via standard Android NotificationListenerService to log new alerts persistently in the background with battery-optimized efficiency.
