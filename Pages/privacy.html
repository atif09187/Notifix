Privacy Policy for Notifox

Last Updated: November 2024 • Developer: Gondal Studios

1. Introduction

This Privacy Policy explains how Notifox ("the Application"), developed by Gondal Studios ("we", "us", or "our"), processes information when you install and use the notification history app on your Android device.

We are committed to operating with complete transparency. We do not make deceptive privacy claims, nor do we state that "no data is ever collected" if third-party advertising SDKs process advertising identifiers.

2. Android Notification Access Permission

Notifox requires special Android system authorization known as Notification Access (via Android’s native NotificationListenerService). This permission is essential to provide notification tracking, WhatsApp notification saving, and notification log functionality.

  • Purpose: Notification Access is utilized solely to receive notifications posted by applications you have permitted Notifox to monitor, enabling notification history viewing, sorting, and organization.
  • Scope: The Application reads notification metadata, including the originating package name, title, timestamp, and message body content delivered to your system tray.
  • Important Limitation: Notifox captures notifications when they arrive while the background listener service is active. It cannot recover notifications or messages delivered before Notifox was installed or while notification access was disabled.

3. Storage Architecture: Local-Only Storage

Notifox is built on a local-only storage architecture:

  • No Remote Notification Server: Notifox does not operate, maintain, or transmit your saved notification contents to any cloud server or remote database owned or controlled by Gondal Studios.
  • Device Database: Your recorded notification history is saved directly inside your device's private application sandbox (using a local SQLite database).
  • Uninstallation Behavior: When you uninstall Notifox from your Android device, all locally stored application data and notification logs are removed immediately by the Android operating system, subject to standard Android system backup preferences configured on your device.

4. Third-Party Advertising and Analytics SDKs

To support ongoing development and maintenance, Notifox may incorporate third-party advertising SDKs (such as Google AdMob or Unity Ads) to serve advertisements within the Application:

  • These third-party advertising networks may automatically collect and process certain non-sensitive device telemetry, including your device's Google Advertising ID (AAID), IP address, device brand and model, operating system version, and ad interaction logs.
  • This information is governed by the respective privacy policies of those third-party providers. We encourage users to review Google's advertising privacy disclosures at policies.google.com/technologies/ads.
  • Important: Third-party advertising SDKs integrated into Notifox do not receive or have access to the contents of your recorded notification history.

5. User Control and Permissions

You maintain full control over the permissions granted to Notifox:

  • You can revoke Notifox's Notification Access at any time through your Android device settings under Settings > Apps & Notifications > Special App Access > Notification Access.
  • Within Notifox, you can clear all recorded history or filter specific applications you do not wish to capture.

6. Security Limitations

While notification logs reside strictly on your physical handset, the security of data saved on the device depends on your device's physical security, Android system lock mechanisms, lock-screen authentication, and whether your device has undergone rooting or unauthorized firmware modifications.
