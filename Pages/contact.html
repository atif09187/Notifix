Frequently Addressed Inquiries

How do I ensure Notifox logs notifications in the background?

Ensure that Notifox has battery optimization set to "Unrestricted" in your Android system settings, as OEM battery managers can suspend background listener services.

Can Notifox recover WhatsApp messages deleted before installation?

No. Notifox preserves notifications captured from the Android status bar in real-time. It cannot access messages from before the app was installed or messages received while notification permissions were disabled.

How do I submit a feature request?

Send an email with the subject line [Notifox Feature Request] detailing your suggested improvement.
